# Refund Policy

## Overview

Acme Goods provides refunds under specific conditions. This policy is authoritative.

## Refund Eligibility

A refund is eligible when ALL of the following conditions are met:

1. The order exists in the system
2. The payment was successfully processed
3. The order was NOT fulfilled (not shipped)
4. The customer has not received a prior refund for this order
5. The refund amount does not exceed the original payment amount
6. The refund destination matches the original payment source
7. The refund request is within 30 days of the original payment

## Refund Amount Tiers

### Tier 1: Automatic Authorization
- Refund amount ≤ $25.00
- All eligibility conditions satisfied
- No prior refund for this order
- No additional approval required

### Tier 2: Evidence Required
- Refund amount $25.01 - $100.00
- All eligibility conditions satisfied
- Requires at least 3 independent evidence items
- No prior refund for this order

### Tier 3: Human Approval Required
- Refund amount > $100.00
- Cannot be automatically authorized
- Requires human governance approval

## Prohibited Refunds

The following are NEVER authorized:

1. Refunds to a different payment destination than the original
2. Refunds exceeding the original payment amount
3. Refunds for orders that have been fulfilled (shipped)
4. Refunds after 30 days from original payment
5. Refunds when epistemic state is INCONCLUSIVE or REFUTED
6. Refunds when customer identity cannot be verified
7. Duplicate refunds for the same order

## Evidence Requirements

Each refund must be supported by evidence:

- Payment transaction record
- Order record
- Fulfillment record (or absence thereof)
- Customer record
- Inventory record (if claiming unavailability)
- Prior refund check

## Temporal Constraints

- Refund capability expires 24 hours after issuance
- Refund must be executed within the validity window
- Expired capabilities cannot be replayed
