# Business Operations

## Company Overview

Acme Goods is a small e-commerce business selling physical products. All operations are local-first.

## Order Lifecycle

1. Customer places order
2. Payment is processed
3. Inventory is checked
4. Order is fulfilled (shipped)
5. Customer receives product

## Key Systems

- Payment processor (simulated)
- Inventory management
- Fulfillment system
- Customer support
- Refund processing
